## 1. What This Website Is For
An online academy landing page doing two jobs at once: teaching children the Quran (reading, Tajweed, and memorization) and providing academic tutoring for Class 1–10 students. One page, one clear goal — get a parent to book a free trial class.

## 2. Who's Visiting This Page
Mostly parents, roughly 28–45 years old, looking for a tutor they can trust with their child. Some are based in Pakistan; others are Muslim families living abroad who want their kids to stay connected to Quran and Urdu. *(This brief is written to serve both audiences — tell me if you'd rather focus on just one, local or international, and I'll tighten the copy.)*

## 3. The One Action You Want Visitors to Take
Book a free trial class. Every section should lead back to this — the main CTA button, a WhatsApp chat bubble, or a short enrollment form.

## 4. Tone of Voice
Warm and sincere, like a teacher speaking to a parent — not a hard sell. Confident about your quality, humble in how you say it. Parents are choosing someone to teach their child, so sincerity matters more than hype.

## 5. Suggested Colors
- **Primary:** Deep green (#0B6E4F) — trust and calm
- **Accent:** Warm gold (#D4A017) — buttons, highlights
- **Background:** Soft cream (#FAF7F0)
- **Text:** Charcoal (#2B2B2B) rather than pure black
- *Optional:* a gentle sky blue for the academic section, so the Class 1–10 content feels a touch more playful next to the Quran section

## 6. Section-by-Section Content

**Header**
Logo + academy name, nav links (Home / Quran Courses / Academic Courses / Teachers / Pricing / Contact), and a "Book Free Trial" button that stays visible as visitors scroll.

**Hero**
- Headline: *"Give Your Child the Gift of Quran & a Strong Education — From Home"*
- Subheadline: *"Live online classes in Quran recitation, Hifz, and Tajweed, plus school subjects for Class 1–10 — taught by caring, qualified tutors, wherever you are."*
- Button: *"Book a Free Trial Class"*
- Image: a child learning at a laptop or tablet with a Quran nearby — warm and real, not overly corporate stock photography

**Trust Bar**
A row of quick numbers once you have them: *"500+ Students" · "Qualified Male & Female Tutors" · "Flexible Time Zones" · "4.9★ Parent Rating."* Small honest numbers beat inflated ones.

**Why Choose Us**
- Qualified, background-checked Quran and academic tutors
- One-on-one or small group classes
- Flexible scheduling across time zones
- Academic tutoring aligned with your child's school syllabus
- Affordable monthly packages
- Free trial class, no card required

**Courses — shown as two clear tracks**
- *Quran Track:* Noorani Qaida for beginners, Quran Reading (Nazra) with Tajweed, Hifz (Memorization) Program, Islamic Studies & daily Duas
- *Academic Track (Class 1–10):* Math, Science, English, Urdu, Islamiyat, Social Studies

**How It Works**
1. Register and tell us your child's current level
2. Get matched with the right tutor
3. Join a free trial class
4. Start regular classes and receive monthly progress updates

**Meet the Teachers**
Photo, name, short bio, and qualification for 3–4 tutors (e.g., *"Hafiza Ayesha — Ijazah in Tajweed, 6 years teaching experience"*). Include both male and female tutors — many parents specifically look for a female teacher for their daughters.

**Pricing**
Three simple tiers: Quran Only, Academic Only, and Combo (mark this one "Most Popular"). Show price per month, classes per week, and class length. Mention how parents can pay — JazzCash/Easypaisa/bank transfer locally, card/PayPal internationally.

**Testimonials**
Two or three short parent quotes with name, city or country, and a star rating — placeholders for now until you collect real ones.

**FAQ**
- What app do you use for classes?
- Are your teachers qualified and verified?
- Can I choose my child's class timing?
- Is the trial class really free?
- What happens if my child misses a class?

**Final Call-to-Action**
Repeat the hero's promise: *"Ready to start your child's journey with the Quran and a stronger education? Book a free trial class today — no obligation."*

**Footer**
Contact details, WhatsApp number, email, social icons, countries served, and quick links.

## 7. Features the Site Needs
- A floating WhatsApp button, always visible — this matters a lot for how parents in this market prefer to reach out
- Fully mobile-responsive, since most parents will browse from their phone
- A short, simple trial-booking form: name, WhatsApp number, child's class/age, preferred class time
- Trust signals (stats, credentials) visible without scrolling
